Method and limitations

Wear is the reported maximum calculated tubing wear (%/year); pump speed is in RPM. Means give equal weight to each well. Mean difference = unlined mean − HDPE mean. Relative difference = mean difference / unlined mean × 100. It is unavailable when the reference mean is zero.

Calculated zeros do not establish absence of physical wear. Existing reported values and model factors are preserved. This view provides descriptive comparisons, not failure dates or safe operating limits. Field membership is not inferred from well names. PIPESIM 2023 is supplementary and no live connection is used.
